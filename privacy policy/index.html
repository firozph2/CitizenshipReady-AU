Privacy Policy

Citizenship Ready AU

Effective date: 21 September 2026

1. Overview

Citizenship Ready AU is an independent educational app designed to help users study for the Australian citizenship test. The app is designed to work primarily offline.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app stores, what it does not collect, and how locally stored study data is handled.

2. Information we collect

Citizenship Ready AU does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as a user's name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, messages or payment information.

The app does not include:

  • advertising SDKs;
  • analytics SDKs;
  • third-party tracking SDKs;
  • cloud account sign-in;
  • cloud synchronisation of quiz progress; or
  • developer-operated servers for storing user quiz data.

3. Study and app-use data stored on your device

To provide study features, the app may store limited information locally on the user's device, such as:

  • quiz progress and results;
  • incorrect-question history used for retry practice;
  • bookmarked questions;
  • mock-test or practice progress; and
  • app preferences such as theme selection.

This information remains on the device and is not intentionally uploaded to the publisher.

4. Data sharing

Citizenship Ready AU does not intentionally sell, rent or share users' locally stored study data with advertisers, data brokers or analytics companies.

The app does not contain advertising or behavioural-tracking functionality in this version.

5. Internet access and external websites

The core study and quiz functions are designed to work offline. If a user chooses to open the Privacy Policy or another external link, the app may open the user's web browser.

This Privacy Policy is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub or the user's browser may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. That processing is outside the direct control of Citizenship Ready AU.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored study data remains on the user's device unless it is cleared by the user, removed through device/app-storage controls, or deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Because the publisher does not intentionally receive or maintain a server-side copy of this local study data, there is no publisher-held account database to delete.

7. Security

Citizenship Ready AU minimises privacy risk by keeping study data locally on the device and by not using advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs in this version. Users should also protect their device with appropriate device security settings.

8. Children's privacy

The app is an educational study tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no user account is required and the app does not intentionally transmit personal study data to the publisher, the app does not maintain profiles of child users.

9. Australian Government source and independence notice

Study summaries, practice questions, answers and explanations in this version are based on the testable material in Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond.

Citizenship Ready AU is an independent study product. It is not an official Australian Government app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Australian Government.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The current version will be published at the Privacy Policy URL used in the app and Google Play listing.
